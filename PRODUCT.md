# Product

## Register

product

## Users

**Fleet Manager (Admin)** — runs the whole fleet for an SMB (logistics, field service, transport) in France. Non-technical, time-pressed, often interrupted. Assigns vehicles, monitors compliance (contrôle technique, carte grise, driver documents), tracks maintenance costs. Needs speed, clarity, zero cognitive overhead.

**Collaborator** — dispatcher or assistant invited into the admin's org with limited write access. Logs day-to-day operations (mileage, washes, assignments) without touching billing or org settings.

Context of use: desktop-first during office hours; occasional mobile checks in the field. French-language market (i18n FR/EN).

## Product Purpose

FleetDesk centralizes vehicle tracking, driver management, maintenance scheduling, and French regulatory compliance for SMB fleets, replacing scattered spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, and legacy desktop tools. Success: a fleet manager sees fleet health at a glance, never misses an inspection or document expiry, and completes daily logging tasks in seconds.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, dependable. Expert confidence without enterprise coldness. The tool of a professional who values their time. Brand blue #0066FF used with restraint.

## References & Anti-references

References: Linear, Stripe Dashboard — sober, fast, controlled density, keyboard-friendly, obvious hierarchy.

Anti-references: overloaded legacy ERPs (Excel-grid walls, ten-deep menus); enterprise fleet suites (Samsara-style complexity); generic bootstrapped admin templates with identical card grids and hero metrics.

## Design Principles

1. **Compliance is the hero.** Expiring documents, inspections, and maintenance due are the highest-value signals; they surface before anything else.
2. **Glanceable, then actionable.** Every screen answers "is anything wrong?" in under 3 seconds, and lets the user act on it in one click.
3. **Entity hierarchy is the map.** Organization → Vehicles → Drivers → Assignments. Navigation mirrors it; detail views are the workhorses.
4. **Logging must be frictionless.** Mileage, washes, assignments are repetitive daily tasks: minimal fields, smart defaults, no modal mazes.
5. **Restraint over decoration.** Density controlled, color meaningful (status, not ornament), no dashboard theater.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: contrast ratios, full keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced-motion respect. French and English locales; dates and numbers localized.
