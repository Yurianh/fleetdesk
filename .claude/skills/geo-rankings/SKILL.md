# GEO rankings

An assistant asked to recommend a tool searches the web for something like
« meilleur logiciel gestion de flotte 2026 » and cites what it finds. So GEO
starts as SEO on **« meilleur + money keyword + year »** — and the source that
counts most is a **third party** listing FleetDesk, not FleetDesk ranking
itself first.

## Steps

1. From `marketing/keywords.md`, take the top 5 commercial keywords.
2. Form the AI query for each: `meilleur <keyword> <current year>`
   (also try `comparatif <keyword>` and `top <keyword>`).
3. For each, find who ranks on page 1 (WebSearch, or ChatSEO if connected)
   and keep the **rankings, comparisons and listicles**.
4. Write `marketing/geo.md`: query · listicle URL · site behind it · are we in
   it · contact (editorial email or form, from the site itself).
5. Draft **one short, personal outreach email per listicle we are missing
   from**: who we are, who FleetDesk is for, what it does differently (French
   compliance, chauffeur entry, 14-day trial without card), and an offer of a
   demo account. No payment offered or asked. **The user sends them
   personally** — never send from this skill.
6. Suggest title changes on our own commercial pages only if they stay
   truthful: a dated comparison page is honest; « meilleur logiciel » in our
   own title is a claim we cannot back. Run any change through `page-brief`
   and `ship-page`.
